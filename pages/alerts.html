Exercises

  1. Handle the alert with page.once('dialog') and assert its message.
  2. Accept and then dismiss the confirm; assert both results.
  3. Answer the prompt with text, and also dismiss it.
  4. Open the modal, fill the email and confirm; then cancel; then close with Escape.
  5. Trigger a toast, assert it, close it manually, and wait for another to disappear.
  6. Accept the cookie banner and assert the cookie value via context.cookies().
