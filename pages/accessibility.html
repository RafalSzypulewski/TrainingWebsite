Exercises

  1. In the good section find everything with getByRole and getByLabel (no test ids).
  2. Try the same on the bad section and note what fails. Which locators still work?
  3. Run @axe-core/playwright on each section and compare the violations.
  4. Submit the good form empty, assert the alert message and aria-invalid.
  5. Operate the good accordion and tabs with the keyboard only (Enter, Arrow keys).
  6. Assert aria-expanded and aria-selected states change.
  7. Use toMatchAriaSnapshot on the good form.
  8. Check the tab order and visible focus with keyboard.press('Tab').
